ADDENDUM to PREREGISTRATION_2026-11-14.txt — added metrics and the multimodal plan
Written 2026-11-19, BEFORE any metric was run on a real transcript. The only runs so
far are unit tests on synthetic text (tests/test_score_comm.py). The original file is
not edited; this file only adds to it.

WHY THIS EXISTS
The original preregistration scored transcripts only. The project's full pipeline
(Stages 1 to 7) also uses diarization, screen state and a vision model, and the
supervisor's notes ask for more than speech: quality and accuracy of content, time
spent, visuals, questions raised and answered, writing on the board, expression,
movement, hand movement, body language, engagement and multimodality. This file says
which of those can be measured now from the transcripts, and which need video that
has not been downloaded yet.

UNCHANGED
M1 to M5, the judge setup, the 12-window hand check, predictions P1 to P7 and the
procedure. The judge stays Qwen2.5 7B Instruct as preregistered. The 14B model used in
Stage 2 of the main pipeline may be run later as a sensitivity check only if it fits
in GPU memory; that has not been tested.

ADDED TRANSCRIPT METRICS (defined in communication/score_comm.py, frozen with it)
  M6  repetition rate: adjacent repeated words or repeated two-word phrases, per 1,000
      words. Whisper drops most "um" and "uh" but keeps many repeats.
  M7  signposting rate: phrases such as "today we", "last time", "to recap",
      "in conclusion", per 1,000 words (organisation).
  M8  MTLD lexical diversity, computed on lectures of at least 50 words.
  M9  pace variability: standard deviation of words per 60-second window.
  Q   questions per 10 minutes: sentences ending "?" in Whisper's punctuation.
  X   example cues per 1,000 words ("for example", "suppose", "imagine").
  V1  visual-dependency rate per 1,000 words ("as you can see", "this equation",
      "on the board", "over here"), the transcript-only deixis detector of Stage 3.
  V2  board and drawing cue rate per 1,000 words ("let me write", "write down",
      "let me draw").
  Phrases are counted once each, longest phrase first, so a stretch of speech is never
  counted twice.
These are descriptive. None gets a prediction, and none is combined into one score.

HOW EACH NOTE IS HANDLED
  Quality of content ........ judge criteria (clarity, organisation, defined terms,
                              examples) plus M7 and X. Now.
  Accuracy of content ....... NOT scored. A 7B model cannot verify physics or
                              economics. Only the F1 content-error flag from rubric
                              v2 is used: the judge may flag a suspected error with
                              a timestamp, and I check every flag by hand.
  Time spent ................ speech fraction, pauses (M4) and pace (M1, M9) now.
                              Time per topic needs Stage 2 segmentation: later.
  Visuals ................... V1 and V2 are proxies for how much a lecture leans on
                              the screen or board. Actual visuals need video.
  Questions raised .......... Q counts questions in the transcript, by anyone.
  Questions answered ........ needs diarization, so it can tell a student's question
                              from the lecturer's. Audio was deleted after
                              transcription, so this needs a re-download.
  Writing on the board ...... V2 now. Real detection needs video frames.
  Expression, movement, hand
  movement, body language,
  engagement ................ video only. Not measured yet.
  Effectiveness ............. cannot be measured. There are no student outcomes, so
                              every measure here describes delivery, and none shows
                              that it helped anyone learn.
  Multimodality ............. needs the video stages above.

VIDEO PILOT (not started)
Download video for six lectures, one per course, and run Stage 1 (screen states),
pose and face landmarks for movement and hand activity, and pyannote diarization.
Follow the TeachObs coding scheme (gesture, board work, pointing, visual materials,
questioning, feedback) at 15-second scenes. Report the pilot as a feasibility result
with no claims about the 103-lecture corpus. Video of 103 lectures is about 116 hours
and is not planned.

REFERENCES CHECKED
  TeachObs (arXiv 2605.30673): 39 binary observation codes per 15-second scene, 20
    visual and 19 non-visual. Used as the coding scheme for the pilot.
  M-Powering Teachers (Stanford EduNLP): NLP feedback on classroom talk, including
    questioning quality. The project page does not define its measures, so none are
    copied here; the cited papers must be read first.
  ClassMind: a lesson-plan and quiz generator, not a measurement tool. Not used.
  LearningCues: could not be identified from the name alone. Not used.
