PRE-REGISTRATION: video stage test on MIT 6.100L Lectures 3, 10 and 17
Written 2026-10-23, before Stage 1 has been run on these videos and before any
language model or vision-language model has seen them.

Background
Under rubric v1, hand scoring placed Lecture 17 (Python Classes) below Lectures 3
(Iteration) and 10 (Lists and Mutability) on Implementation because Lecture 17
never runs its code. The transcript measures could not see this (report of 2026-10-07).
Rubric v2 splits the dimension; IM2 is "artifact executed or verified".

Predictions
P1  Stage 1 change detection. On a spot-check of 20 randomly chosen detected
    changes per lecture, at least 16 (80%) are real screen changes.
    Fails if fewer than 16 are real in any lecture.

P2  Stage 3, IM2. The VLM marks at least one screen state as showing program
    output or a console in Lecture 3 and in Lecture 10, and none in Lecture 17.
    Fails if Lecture 3 or 10 has no such state, or if Lecture 17 has one that a
    human check confirms shows real output. (If a human check confirms real
    output in Lecture 17, the v1 hand score was wrong, and that is reported.)

P3  Condition comparison on IM2. With video, Lecture 17 scores strictly below
    both Lectures 3 and 10. With transcript only, it does not (tie or inversion).
    Fails if the transcript-only judge already places Lecture 17 strictly below
    both, in which case video adds nothing on this case and that is reported.
